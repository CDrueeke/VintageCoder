Origin of the character sets in this folder
===========================================

These files are VintageCoder's own standard character sets "VC-Standard".
Author: the VintageCoder project. License: CC0-1.0 (public domain, freely redistributable, also in your own programs).
No original ROMs and no replicas of original characters were used.
The design is a rule-based draft; the release of the glyph shapes is still pending.

Sets:
  vc-mega65-upper (revision 1, license CC0-1.0): VC-Standard MEGA65 Upper/Graphics (full color mode)
