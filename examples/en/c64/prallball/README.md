# PRALLBALL (C64) - Prototype H1

Ball-and-paddle game in assembler; first playable state.
The source is deliberately commented in detail.

- **Build:** `vcc build .` (or IDE F7), start `build/prallball.prg` in VICE `x64sc`. Test with the computer as player: `vcc asm prallball.asm -D AUTOPLAY=1 -o autoplay.prg`.
- **Controls:** joystick port 2 or keyboard (Z left, X right, space fire, RUN/STOP abort).
- **State (phase P1, C64 complete except paddle):** 24 levels (20 regular, **2 bonus** with 45 s time, +100 per brick and no ball loss, **2 boss** with cores that need several hits and throw evil capsules every 4 s), bounce angle by hit position (8 zones) plus a small influence of the paddle movement (one zone), a side hit on the paddle deflects the ball sideways (no jumping up), silver/gold/indestructible bricks, 16 speed levels, **rotating coin capsules** (12 x 12 px, rotation around the horizontal axis) (recognisable by colour: light blue wide, light red narrow, red reversed controls, green slow, yellow fast, orange sticky, white extra ball, turquoise points, violet multiball up to 3 balls, light green laser 20 s, **grey pierce 5 s**: the yellow ball destroys every destructible brick at once and does not bounce; # and boss cores still bounce it), high-score list with name entry (**typing + RETURN** or joystick, DEL deletes, the default "SPIELER" disappears at the first character; auto-confirm after 30 s) and saving, sound effects, **title music** (80 bars, D minor, melody/bass/drums with envelopes, filter, PWM; only on the title and in the demo), **demo mode** (after 30 s idle, without sound effects, levels 1-5).
- **Graphics:** multicolor bricks/walls with light and shadow edge, **22 stable background bands**, visible only in the playfield (column 0 and 39 black) (the raster interrupt waits for the start of the line: clean edges) with four gradients in turn, multicolor sprites, highlight over the bricks, edge flashes, title with block logo (rainbow colour change), scroll text (smooth offset by raster) and bouncing balls.
- **Title/demo ticker:** the subtitle (title, row 10) and the header line of the demo (row 0, instead of the score) change every 4 s between "PRALLE ACTION FUER DEN C64", "EIN DEMOSPIEL VON VINTAGECODER" and (demo only) "*** PRALLBALL ***". Transitions pixel by pixel via self-calculated characters: scroll away up/down, letter by letter, scroll away left/right, wipe, dissolve; plus a rainbow colour.
- **Memory:** screen/character set/sprites are in VIC bank 1 ($4000-$7FFF), the code has $0801-$3FFF.
- **Tests:** `python3 tools/gen_tests.py` creates `prallball.vctest` (55 tests: BCD points, random, direction table, paddle bounce, brick collision, all 24 levels, high-score list incl. CRC); `vcc build . && vcc test . --backend vice`. The test found an error in `hs_check` (equal score).
- **Not yet:** paddle controller in the game (query prepared, untested), input tests (joystick/keyboard cannot be set in the emulator).
- **Test versions:** `-D AUTOPLAY=1` (computer plays endlessly), `-D TESTLOSE=1` (loses on purpose: game over/high-score list), `-D TESTCAP=1` (every brick drops a capsule), `-D TESTMULTI=1` (multiball at once), `-D TESTLASER=1` (laser always on), `-D STARTLEVEL=n` (start with level n+1), `-D TESTFPS=1` (frame counter in row 24: shows skipped frames), `-D NOTICKER=1` (demo without ticker in the header line).
- **Libraries:** `examples/c64/prallball/lib/input.asm`, `hiscore.asm`, `rand.asm`, `sound.asm` (contract: see the header comments of the files).
- **Levels:** drafts in `levels/levels.txt`; `python3 tools/make_levels.py` creates drafts 4-24 (`levels/levels.txt`), `python3 tools/gen.py` creates from them `lib/levels.inc`, `lib/dirtable.inc` and the graphics data `lib/gfx.inc.*`; `python3 tools/compose.py` the title music `lib/music.inc`.

## Settings and demo player (new)

At the start of `prallball.asm` there is the block **EINSTELLUNGEN** (settings; can also be set with `-D NAME=value` when assembling):
`EXTRA_RATE` (40: chance in 256 per destroyed brick of capsules; about twice as many as before), `DEMO_REAKTION_MIN`/`DEMO_REAKTION_MAX` (4/12 frames of reaction time after a change of direction of the ball), `DEMO_FEHLER_RATE`, `DEMO_FEHLER_FERN`, `DEMO_UNGENAU`, `DEMO_VERFEHLEN_RATE`, `DEMO_EXTRA_LUST`, `DEMO_EXTRA_FERN`.
The demo player thus acts more human (reaction time, short mistakes, inaccurate hits, rare deliberate misses) and catches good falling capsules as long as the ball is far away.
Test version `-D AUTOPLAY=1 -D TESTDEMO=1` counts events of the demo player in RAM (see header comment).

**Angle randomness (new):** On each paddle hit the bounce angle is shifted by exactly one zone (17 degrees) to the left or right with the chance `WINKEL_ZUFALL` (default 64 of 256, about every fourth hit). This applies to the human player and to the demo player; the ball always stays within the eight zones, and at the edges it is not pushed beyond them. It prevents endless, identical ball paths. `0` switches it off (`-D WINKEL_ZUFALL=0`). With `-D AUTOPLAY=1` the computer now plays all levels without end (before: only the first five); `-D TESTDEMO=1` additionally counts the angle shifts, the paddle hits, the completed levels, the destroyed bricks and the levels ended by the time limit (addresses in the header of `prallball.asm`).

**Demo time limit (new):** If the demo player hits no brick for `DEMO_STEIN_LIMIT` seconds (default 40), the level counts as completed and the next one begins, so an unreachable or tough last brick cannot hold the demo up endlessly. Only in the demo (and with `AUTOPLAY`), never in the game for humans; `0` switches it off.
