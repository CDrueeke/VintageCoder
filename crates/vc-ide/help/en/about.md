# About VintageCoder

The card **About VintageCoder** is on the page **Info and update** of the settings. It shows the name and version number, the license (GPL-3.0-or-later; plus a short note that your outputs are free to use, see [License and acknowledgements](help:license)) and the author. The section **Acknowledgements** names included third-party parts with name, author, license and link (the link opens only on click); see [License and acknowledgements](help:license). The page also has the buttons **Website**, **Source code** and **Feedback**, the section **Updates** and the card **Third parties** with **Show notices** (the third-party notices, `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES`).

How to open it:

- Menu **Help > About VintageCoder** (on the Mac also in the menu **VintageCoder**)
- Command palette (Cmd/Ctrl+K): "About VintageCoder"
- Gear ⚙ or Cmd/Ctrl+Comma, then **Info and update** on the left

The buttons **Website** and **Source code** open only when you click them; the page itself does not fetch anything from the network ("This page never fetches anything from the network on its own.").

Report errors and wishes with **Help > Send feedback ...**, see [Send feedback](help:feedback); the page has its own button for this. You can see whether a newer version exists in the section **Updates** of the page (button **Check for updates now**), see [Check for updates](help:updates).
