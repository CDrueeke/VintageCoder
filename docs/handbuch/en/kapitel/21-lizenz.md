# License and acknowledgements

**Goal:** You know in short what you may do with VintageCoder and the files you create with it, and whom VintageCoder owes parts to.

## The essentials

1. **What you build with VintageCoder belongs to you.** You may use and pass on programs, source code, disk images, graphics and music freely, also without mentioning VintageCoder.
2. **The VCASM language and the file formats are free.** Anyone may implement them in their own tools.
3. **VintageCoder itself** is under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later, without warranty. The text is in the file **LICENSE**; details are in **LICENSING.md** (in the installation folder under `share/doc` or `Resources`).

System files, templates and example projects are under **MIT-0**: you may take them into your own programs without conditions. The name "VintageCoder" and the logo are excluded from the GPL; details are in LICENSING.md.

## Acknowledgements

VintageCoder contains parts of other projects. The full list with names, authors, licenses and links is under **Settings > Info and update** in the section **Acknowledgements** and in the file `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`. This includes the emulators that VintageCoder starts and that you install yourself (VICE, atari800, Altirra, Fujisan, Xemu) and the programs and formats from which VintageCoder reads files (GoatTracker 2, the SID file format with the High Voltage SID Collection, SpritePad); these programs are not included. One example is the **RMT player** in the Atari examples `music_demo` and `jumprun`: [Raster Music Tracker](https://github.com/raster-atari-org/RASTER-Music-Tracker) by Radek Sterba / Raster / C.P.U., continued by Vin Samuel and Peter Dell, GPL v3.

## The notice when building

If a source file has a copyleft license (GPL, LGPL, AGPL) in its header, the notice **L001** appears when you build. It is not an error. More is in [License notice when building](hilfe:license-build).
